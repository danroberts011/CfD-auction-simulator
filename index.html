Competitor bids use a split-normal approximation calibrated to P25/P50/P75. Government budget or willingness to pay is also uncertain and is drawn once per simulated auction. Your bid is fixed. Your own minimum acceptable bid is uncertain and is drawn from its P25/P50/P75 distribution when you win. Projects are ranked by implied £/MWh. In budget mode, accepted project bids count against the sampled £m budget.
